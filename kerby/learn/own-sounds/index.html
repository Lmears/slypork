Practical guide

Use your own kick and bass sounds

Design the sounds in another synth, then let KerBy play them with its rhythm and effects.

  1. Design your sound and drop it in.

    Make your kick or bass however you want it to sound, then drop it on that voice’s Wave tab.

  2. Play C0 to hear it at its root.

    C0 plays the sound as you made it. Other notes change its pitch and length.

  3. Pitch and Filter switch off.

    Dropping a sample on Wave turns that voice’s Pitch and Filter tabs off, since a sample usually has its own pitch and filter movement. Turn them back on if you want KerBy’s on top.

  4. Play it.

    Hold a note and listen to your sound running on KerBy’s pulses. Rate, the step row, Glitch, Amp, the duck, Reverse, Tone, Crush and the kick’s reverb all work on it.

Keep the kick at one pitch

Close the padlock at the bottom right of the kick’s Wave plot. Every note then plays the kick as you recorded it, in any scale, while the bass still follows the notes.

Keep the drawn kick instead

To keep KerBy’s kick and layer a sample over it, use the Click tab. Add an attack with a sample covers it.
