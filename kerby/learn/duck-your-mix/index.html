Practical guide · Ableton Live

Duck the rest of your mix to the kick

Duck any track or group to KerBy’s kick: put DucKy on it and set it to follow KerBy. No MIDI routing needed.

  1. Put DucKy on what you want to duck.

    Load DucKy, free, on a track or group and choose KerBy.

  2. Play KerBy.

    That’s it: play a pattern on KerBy, and DucKy ducks on every kick, even as you change the kick’s Rate or turn up Glitch.

    With more than one KerBy, right-click KerBy in DucKy and choose one by its track. The one you’re playing says playing.

Mute the kick, lose the duck

A muted kick doesn’t duck anything either. To keep the pump without hearing the kick, turn the kick down on its track instead.

Using another ducker

Any MIDI-triggered ducker works from KerBy’s MIDI out, such as ShaperBox, LFOTool or Devious Machines’ Duck. In Live, use KerBy’s VST3: Live only takes MIDI out from the VST3. Add an empty MIDI track and set its MIDI From to KerBy’s track, then KerBy in the menu below, the third option under Pre FX and Post FX.

Set its Monitor to In, and its MIDI To to the ducker’s track, then the ducker in the menu below. Switch the ducker’s trigger to MIDI.
