Practical guide

Add an attack with a sample

Keep the kick’s synthesised body and borrow the attack from a recording.

  1. Drop a sample on the Click tab.

    Open the kick’s Click tab and drop in a short sound with an attack you like.

  2. Trim it to the attack.

    Drag the Start grip, at the plot’s bottom left, to where the attack begins. Then shape the envelope so the rest falls away.

  3. Keep the low end in the kick.

    Use HP to cut the sample’s low end. Bring up the Click knob with the whole kick playing, and stop before the sample takes over the hit.

  4. Choose whether it follows the notes.

    Close the padlock to keep the attack the same on every note, or open it to follow the keys.

Using a sample as the whole voice

Drop it onto Wave instead. This switches off the Pitch tab, and the bass’s Filter tab too. Tick either back on when you want it.
