Practical guide

Find the space between the hits

A long kick’s tail runs into the bass. For more space between them, end the kick sooner or start the bass later.

  1. Start with a long kick.

    Hold a note on Init, as in your first pattern. On the kick’s Amp tab, raise Length a little.

  2. See where they meet.

    Click the + between the scopes to merge them: the kick in pink, the bass in purple, and a bold white line showing their combined signal.

    Overlap isn’t necessarily a problem. Auto-align is on by default and adjusts the bass’s phase against the kick’s tail to reduce cancellation. Listen to how much space you want between them. Keep the overlap for a fuller handover, or shorten the kick or bring the bass in later for more separation. Right-click the bass’s Phase value to turn Auto-align on or off.

  3. If it needs more space, pick a side.

    End the kick sooner: on the kick’s Amp tab, bring Length down.

    Or start the bass later: on the bass’s Duck tab, drag the curve’s rise later.

Or swap the bass for rumble

Mute the bass. On the kick’s More tab, turn up Reverb Amt with Size up and LP down. The tail follows the same duck curve.
