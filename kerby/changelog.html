v1.2.0 · 2026-11-10

KerBy is a kick and bass instrument: you hold a single note and you get a kick and bass groove. This is its first public release, and this is what's in it.

Playing it

  • Live and Draw. In Live, a held note repeats as Kick and Bass pulses in time with your DAW. In Draw, each note you write is one hit of both voices.
  • A Rate knob on each voice, for how often it pulses.
  • Return Home puts the rates, the wheels and any control set to go home back where they were. Press I, or map it to a button. To add a knob, tick "Goes home" in its right-click menu.
  • Glitch, on each voice's More tab, mangles the pulses on the grid.
  • The Bass step row, for leaving gaps, changing notes and stretching steps, over a pattern from one step to two bars long. Randomize is in its right-click menu.
  • Lock both voices to a scale from the header. Route an LFO to Tune for a bassline that stays in key.
  • A Play button in the header gets a groove going with one click.
  • Play it from a game controller, on macOS. Tested with a PS5 controller.

The sound

  • Draw your own wave or drop in a sample, mono or stereo, on either voice's Wave tab.
  • Samples follow your notes from C0. Close the tab's padlock to pin one to its original pitch.
  • Amp, Pitch and Filter envelopes for each hit.
  • A Click layer on the Kick, for layering a sample over it.
  • FM, saturation, noise and downsampling, and a reverb on the Kick for a low rumble.
  • Drag a bar from any scope into your DAW as audio, or back onto a voice to play it as a sample.

Sidechain

  • Auto-align, on the right-click menu of the Bass's Phase: every Bass hit starts in phase with the Kick's tail, whatever note you play, to prevent phase cancellation. It also compensates for the phase shift caused by filtering.
  • The Bass ducks under the Kick automatically, on a curve you can redraw.
  • Merge the scopes to see where the two overlap.
  • MIDI out, one short note per Kick pulse, for sidechaining anything to the Kick. In Live, use the VST3 for it.
  • DucKy, a free ducking plugin, pay what you want, ducks the rest of your mix to KerBy's Kick with no routing: ducky.slypork.net

Modulation

  • A Matrix page. Press N, or click the page icons in the footer. Eight macros, eight LFOs, both voices' envelopes, velocity and both wheels can each drive nearly any control, in up to 32 routes.
  • Drag a macro's name or an LFO's label onto a control to route it, or right-click the control and choose "Modulate with".
  • Morph an LFO or a voice's wave from your drawing into the preset shapes: drag a macro or another LFO onto its plot.

Presets

  • 30 factory presets: kick and bass grooves, drums and FX.

What it runs on

  • macOS 11 or later, Apple Silicon or Intel: VST3 and AU.
  • Windows 10 or later, 64-bit: VST3.
  • Any DAW that loads VST3 or AU instruments. There's no AAX version, so it won't run in Pro Tools.

Updates

  • Updates are free. Your download link always has the latest build, and everyone who bought KerBy gets an email when there's a new one.

Changes since the last beta (1.1.0)

  • Eight LFOs and 32 routes on the Matrix page, twice what there were. The + beside the LFOs adds the next one, up to eight.
  • The controller icon gives one brief blink for each button press, stick push or trigger squeeze.
  • A Play button in the header gets a groove going with one click. Any key moves its note and letting go of the key doesn't stop it. On a one-shot preset it plays one hit.
  • Duck Rate on the Duck tab is one value you drag up for faster and down for slower, in place of the /2 and x2 buttons either side of it. Click it to pick from the list, or double-click it to go back to 1/4.
  • Cross on a PS5 controller works as the Play button does: either starts or stops the same note, and your keys move it as the D-pad does. It plays one hit on a one-shot preset.
  • Wave and Click samples now follow your notes from C0. Close either tab's padlock to pin that sample to its original pitch.
  • Scale lock no longer changes a sample's pitch when Key is set to a note.
  • Stereo samples retain their left and right channels in Wave and Click.
  • The keyboard has no octave labels. Hover a key to see its note and frequency; Key's tooltip shows the same for a pinned note.
  • A flat line in Draw mode leaves an anchor at each end, not a pair at every gridline.
  • Draw mode changes only the cells you draw: the shape beside them stays as it was, with no slope pulled across it.
  • Draw mode's steps stay square when the view is zoomed in.
  • Random Steps and Random Curves fill the view when it's zoomed in.
  • The scopes draw the same hit the same way every time, up to its true peak.
  • The waveform behind Amp, Pitch and Filter draws out as a note rings, like the scope does.
  • A PS5 controller plays the KerBys whose windows are open, so it follows the track you select in Live, and plays both layers of a rack together.
  • A random LFO's levels land on its grid's Y lines, as its steps follow X: at Y 1 it jumps between the bottom and the top alone. Presets saved before this open at Y 32.
  • Length goes up to 200%, two bars, so a Bass can last all the way to the next Kick at 2/1. Length automation drawn in an older project plays back different.
  • Matrix: choosing an LFO that isn't showing yet as a route's source scrolls the LFO row to its new panel, the way the + does.
  • Phase on each voice's Wave tab and on every LFO: look for a 0° in their respective strips.
  • The Wave tab's padlock works on a drawn wave too. Closing it pins Key on the More tab to C0, and opening it sets Key back to MIDI. The Kick's padlock and the Click's are independent, so a pinned Kick can have a Click that follows your notes.
  • Presets: Kick & Bass is the first Factory folder, ahead of Drums and FX, so Next from Init lands on a kick-and-bass patch.
  • Link to Rate, in Length's right-click menu: keep each hit's length proportional as you turn Rate.
  • Auto-align, on the right-click menu of the Bass's Phase: every Bass hit starts in phase with the Kick's tail, whatever note you play, to prevent phase cancellation. It also compensates for the phase shift caused by filtering.

v1.0.0 · 2026-10-28

Added

  • New factory presets, including a Drums folder.
  • A modulator can restart on every Bass step.
  • Straight only, for Rate: right-click the Rate knob and pick it, and the knob sweeps 1/4, 1/8, 1/16 with no dotted divisions between them.
  • The Kick's scope shows its reverb in red.
  • Shift-click a shape's line to add a point where it crosses a gridline.

Changed

  • With a scale on, Tune, Key and the step row show the values you set, and the scale lock moves the note as it plays.
  • Dragging an off step up or down switches it on.
  • Hovering a shape's line previews the point a click will add.

v0.11.0 · 2026-10-23

Added

  • Undo and redo in KerBy's window, with Cmd/Ctrl+Z and Cmd/Ctrl+Shift+Z.
  • Pinch to zoom the shapes and the scopes, and scroll sideways to move along. With a mouse, Cmd/Ctrl+scroll zooms and Shift+scroll moves along.
  • KerBy installs on macOS 10.14 Mojave and 10.15 Catalina as well as macOS 11 and later.

Changed

  • The combined scope shows where the Kick and Bass overlap: each is its own line, and their mix is drawn bold wherever the overlap changes the sound.
  • Duck Smooth leaves the slopes you drew alone, so turning it up no longer delays the release. A saved patch with Smooth up will sound a little different.
  • Glide also slides between Tune changes, so a modulator on Tune glides from note to note.
  • Route amounts into a pitch read in semitones: type 12st for an octave.

Fixed

  • A modulator drawn in steps on Tune plays the same notes every time.
  • With Rate off and Quantize on, taps keep a dotted Rate's spacing.
  • With Rate off, Amp, Pitch and Filter show the whole shape undimmed.
  • A stopped scope shows the bar as you heard it, notes held past their step included.

v0.9.0 · 2026-09-24

Added

  • A Matrix page, for modulation. Open it from the pair of icons in the footer, or press N. Eight macros, four modulators you draw, both voices' envelopes, velocity and both wheels can each drive nearly any control, in up to sixteen routes.
  • Drag a macro's name or a modulator's label onto a control to route it, or right-click the control and choose Modulate with. A routed knob shows a small handle while you hover it; drag that to set how far the route moves it.
  • A modulator's Shape morphs from your drawing through the fixed shapes, and so does each voice's Wave: route something to Wave Shape to sweep the oscillator.
  • Three presets in a new Modulation folder to start from.
  • Glitch, on each voice's More tab, for beat-synced rolls and dropouts. A Kick roll that repeats the hit also ducks the Bass and sends the Kick's MIDI note out.
  • A Filter tab on the Kick, with the Bass's lowpass, fully open to start. Resonance rises as the cutoff comes down.
  • Smooth, on the Duck tab, turns jumps in the duck curve into ramps.
  • The step row's pattern runs from one step to two bars long, set with the − and + at its ends. Drag a step's right edge to stretch it into a longer note.
  • Any knob can go home with Return Home, and so can Reverse and Launch with Kick.
  • KerBy asks to save unsaved changes before you browse to another preset.

Changed

  • Triplet and Restart on Kick have moved to the Rate knob's right-click menu.
  • Reso self-oscillates at 100%. Patches with Reso up may need it turned down.
  • Free Rate gives every doubling of speed the same travel, from 0.1Hz to 100Hz.
  • The Kick reverb no longer turns the Kick down as you raise it.
  • Reverb LP reaches down to 100Hz and cuts more steeply.

Fixed

  • B and Esc work for Draw mode after you click a knob or value box.
  • Raising FM Amt from 0 during a note no longer leaves a DC offset.
  • A reopened session shows its preset's name.

v0.8.0 · 2026-09-17

Added

  • A signed installer for macOS. Run the installer and choose the VST3 or the AU; the Terminal step from earlier drops is no longer needed.
  • Drag a scope out as audio. The bar on screen, or the quarter bar when zoomed, drops into your DAW or back onto a Wave tab as a WAV.
  • Return Home, in the bottom bar, sends both Rate knobs home and both bends to centre. Right-click a Rate knob to set its home.

Changed

  • Everything below 120Hz is summed to mono, on each voice.
  • The Kick reverb's Size gets tighter below 25%.
  • Switching a Rate to Free keeps the rhythm, starting from the division's speed at the current tempo.
  • A scope keeps the last bar played on screen after the voice stops.
  • Anchor points are smaller, and hidden in Draw mode.

Fixed

  • Turning Rate back on while a note is held brings its pulses back.

v0.7.0 · 2026-09-08

Added

  • A Click tab on the Kick layers a second one-shot over every pulse. Drag and drop a sample to get started.
  • Live and Draw modes, in the header. Live turns on quantization and rate division for live play. Draw mode is the opposite, intended for pre-drawn MIDI clips. A bypass box has been added to each rate knob to achieve this.
  • Triplet, in each Rate value's dropdown, swaps the dotted divisions for triplets. VST3 hosts can show either division; some AU hosts may keep the dotted label when toggling.
  • Draw mode in every shape editor. Press B and drag to paint one step per grid cell; Esc or B leaves the mode.
  • A folded corner marks a step box whose finer steps disagree. Rest the pointer on it to open the stored pattern underneath.

Changed

  • The Wave tab's sample trim now has Start only. End control was overkill when we already have Length.

Fixed

  • Holding or releasing Cmd/Ctrl during a value drag no longer jumping occasionally
  • The Kick reverb's Size now reaches a genuinely short room.

v0.6.0 · 2026-09-06

Added

  • Drop an audio file on a voice's Wave tab and that voice plays it as a one-shot instead of its drawn waveform, retriggered by the same pulse clock.
  • A step row under the Bass masks which of its pulses actually sound, and tunes them. Drag a box up or down for ±24 semitones on that step. Show the row from the bottom bar.
  • Key, on both voices' More tabs — pins that voice to one note.
  • Crush, on both voices' More tabs. Sample rate reduction.
  • A bypass box on the Pitch, Filter and Duck tabs.

Changed

  • The More tab's knobs are bracketed into named runs.
  • Reset window size is now an item in the right-click scale menu.

Fixed

  • Turning Rate to a faster division while a note is held speeds the pulse up immediately, rather than waiting up to four beats for the slower rate's already-scheduled pulse to arrive first.
  • Sweeping a free-running Rate in Hz now accelerates smoothly, rather than lurching the other way on some pulses.
  • Spread no longer eats headroom under FM.
  • Noise follows Spread, widening with the voice instead of staying centred.

v0.5.0 · 2026-08-22

Added

  • Grid divisions on the shape editor's strip, X and Y, between the zoom bar and Length. They rule the gridlines and set what Shift+drag snaps to.
  • Random Steps and Random Curves, at the foot of the shape editor's right-click menu. Both draw one step per X division with every level on a Y gridline.

Changed

  • Letting go of the tempo wheel puts the pattern back on the grid at once, rather than walking it there over the following bar. Walk back, at the bottom of the wheel's right-click menu, ramps it there over the next bar instead, with Quantize on.
  • Bending a curve in the shape editor is Option/Alt+drag, not Cmd/Ctrl.

v0.3.0 · 2026-08-18

Added

  • A tempo wheel, at the right-hand end of the keyboard. Right-click for range.
  • Reverb on the Kick — Amt, LP and Size on its More tab, ducked by the Bass's Sidechain curve.
  • Reverse, the new R in the header. It records one Kick rate period and plays it back reversed.
  • MIDI output — one short note per Kick pulse, for sidechaining anything to the Kick. In Live this is VST3 only; other DAWs untested.
  • A combined scope. The + between the two scopes merges them into one panel showing the summed output.

  • Muting the Bass no longer greys out its Duck tab while that curve is still shaping the Kick's reverb.
  • A level meter's clipping cap grows with the overshoot.
  • The logo button lists every shortcut and mouse gesture in the plugin.
  • The scope grows with the window.

Fixed

  • Hovering an envelope point takes the same colour as hovering a line.
