KerBy

Kick and bass,
built for playing.

Hold a note to get a pattern going, or write each hit in your DAW. Draw your own sounds, bring in samples, and shape how the two voices fit together.

Start with a rhythm

Hold a note. Or place every hit.

Play a pattern

Hold a MIDI note and KerBy repeats it as kick and bass pulses, in time with your DAW. Each voice’s Rate knob sets how often it pulses. Tweak the rate for stutters and rhythmic variations, then press the home button to return to the beat.

Write it in

Write the rhythm in your DAW’s piano roll. Each MIDI note triggers both kick and bass.

Leave gaps or change notes in the bass step row, or turn up Glitch to break the pulse into rolls.

Give it a sound

Draw a wave. Drop a sample.

Shape each hit with amplitude, pitch and filter curves. The kick’s Click layer adds a sampled attack on top.

Push it further with FM, saturation, noise and downsampling, or a low rumble from the kick’s reverb.

Bring them together

Sidechain, built in.

The bass ducks under every kick, so the two never fight over the low end.

DucKy, a free ducking plugin, ducks any other track or group to KerBy’s kick.

Make it move

Macros, LFOs and your own shapes.

One macro can reshape the kick and bass together. Lock both voices to a scale, then route a random LFO to the bass’s Tune for a bassline that stays in key.

The voices are called Kick and Bass, but the sounds and patterns can go well beyond either.
