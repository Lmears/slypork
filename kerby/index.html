KerBy

KerBy is a one-stop shop for kick and bass generation I've been building. Two voices, with an opinion about how they relate: the Kick is the backbone, the Bass gets ducked out of its way, and neither ends up off the grid unless you ask it to. It's set up for kicks and basslines, but can be used for much more.

Every envelope in it is a curve — amp, pitch, filter, the duck, and the oscillator waveform itself. Start from a preset shape or draw your own. Hold a note and the clock plays the pattern for you, or feed it a rhythm and it follows — live with your hands on the knobs, or as clips you launch or draw in.

Closed beta · v0.3.0

How it works:

Two voices, one clock. Each runs at its own division, and the Bass restarts on every Kick pulse so the two read as one figure — switch that off and it free-runs. Clip length counts too: a dotted Kick under a one-bar clip resets each bar instead of drifting across them.

The Bass is ducked by the Kick. The duck is a curve you draw, applied over a fixed quarter note after each Kick pulse, so it behaves the same at any Kick rate. A Duck amount takes it from off to full.

Everything is drawn. Amp, pitch, filter, the duck and the waveform all share one editor. Click to add a point, drag to move it, Cmd-drag to bend a segment.

On the grid, when you want it. Quantize (Q) keeps the notes snapped to the grid. Launch with Kick (K) starts both voices together, instead of letting the faster one in first.

What's new:

v0.3.0 · 2026-08-18

Added

  • A tempo wheel, at the right-hand end of the keyboard. It springs back on release but leaves the pattern where the bend put it. Right-click for range.
  • Reverb on the Kick — Amt, LP and Size on its More tab, ducked by the Bass's Sidechain curve.
  • Reverse, the new R in the header. It records one Kick rate period and plays it back reversed, so the hits return in reverse order, not just each hit backwards.
  • MIDI output — one short note per Kick pulse. Drives a compressor's MIDI trigger, a sampler, a gate. In Live it needs the VST3; the AU can't route MIDI out there.
  • A combined scope. The + between the two scopes merges them into one panel showing the summed output, tinted by which voice is responsible for it.
  • Both bends are parameters as well as wheels, so a controller with no bend wheel can reach them. Map the two to one macro for a tape stop.

Changed

  • The scope gridlines sit on real pulses, which fixes dotted rates and unquantized note-ons. They also re-flow onto each Kick pulse, which makes Restart on Kick visible for the first time.
  • The Wave tab dims the part of the cycle a pulse never reaches — a low note under a fast Rate doesn't get all the way round the drawn waveform.
  • Muting the Bass no longer greys out its Duck tab while that curve is still shaping the Kick's reverb.
  • A level meter's clipping cap grows with the overshoot, instead of reading the same at 0.1dB over and 18dB over.
  • Shortcuts & gestures (the logo button) now covers the mouse gestures too.
  • The scope grows with the window, instead of staying a fixed width.

Fixed

  • Hovering an envelope point looked like it would add one. It now previews the move.
Earlier versions

v0.2.1 · 2026-08-13

Added

  • Free-running rate. Each voice's Rate can run in Hz instead of a note division — right-click the Rate knob to switch.

v0.2.0 · 2026-08-13

Added

  • An on-screen keyboard, with a pitch wheel. Play without wiring up MIDI, and watch the notes your DAW sends light up as they arrive. Pitch bend from the host or a hardware wheel works now too.
  • A bottom bar, and a tooltip on every control.
  • Bend, on the Bass — a continuous pitch offset, on its More tab.
  • Interface scale, 75–200%. Right-click the background or the resize corner.

Changed

  • The window reflows instead of scaling. Width and height drag independently and the extra pixels go to the shape editor, so a bigger window buys resolution for placing points rather than bigger knobs. Double-click the resize corner to reset it.
  • Value boxes are typeable and draggable. Tap one and type a number, drag it like a fader, hold Cmd to fine-adjust, or double-click to reset to the default.
  • Length moved out of the knob column onto the shape editor's control strip.
  • Dragging a knob hides and pins the cursor, so a long throw can't run the pointer off the control.

Fixed

  • Resonance self-oscillated slightly off-pitch.
