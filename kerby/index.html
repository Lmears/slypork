Kick and bass,

Start with a rhythm

Hold a note. Or place every hit.

Live Play a pattern

Hold a MIDI note and each voice repeats at its own rate, in time with your DAW. Rate is the main expression control, and the only knob that stays visible on every tab. Turn it up for a brief repeat, then return to the pulse.

Draw Write it in

Write the rhythm in your DAW’s piano roll. Each MIDI note triggers both kick and bass. Let the note last long enough for the bass to come through after the initial duck.

For more variation, use the bass step row to leave gaps and change the pitch of individual steps.

Give it a sound

Draw a wave. Drop a sample.

Start from a preset or draw the waveform itself. Shape the hit with amplitude and pitch curves, then sculpt the bass with its filter.

Drop an audio file onto either voice to use a sample instead. The kick’s separate Click layer lets you add a sampled attack on top.

Push it further with FM, saturation, noise and downsampling, or explore a low rumble with the kick’s reverb.

Bring them together

Shape the kick and bass together.

Change the kick’s length, the bass rhythm or the way one gives way to the other, and hear what it does to the whole groove. Ducking is on by default, and the timing controls let the bass follow the kick or run at its own pace.

Those starting choices make it easy to begin playing. They’re also yours to change: draw a different ducking curve, let the rhythms drift apart, or turn the duck off. The voices are called Kick and Bass, but the sounds and patterns can go well beyond either.

What's new:

v0.7.0 · 2026-09-08

Added

  • A Click tab on the Kick layers a second one-shot over every pulse. Drag and drop a sample to get started.
  • Live and Draw modes, in the header. Live turns on quantization and rate division for live play. Draw mode is the opposite, intended for pre-drawn MIDI clips. A bypass box has been added to each rate knob to achieve this.
  • Triplet, in each Rate value's dropdown, swaps the dotted divisions for triplets. VST3 hosts can show either division; some AU hosts may keep the dotted label when toggling.
  • Draw mode in every shape editor. Press B and drag to paint one step per grid cell; Esc or B leaves the mode.
  • A folded corner marks a step box whose finer steps disagree. Rest the pointer on it to open the stored pattern underneath.

Changed

  • The Wave tab's sample trim now has Start only. End control was overkill when we already have Length.

Fixed

  • Holding or releasing Cmd/Ctrl during a value drag no longer jumping occasionally
  • The Kick reverb's Size now reaches a genuinely short room.
Earlier versions

v0.6.0 · 2026-09-06

Added

  • Drop an audio file on a voice's Wave tab and that voice plays it as a one-shot instead of its drawn waveform, retriggered by the same pulse clock.
  • A step row under the Bass masks which of its pulses actually sound, and tunes them. Drag a box up or down for ±24 semitones on that step. Show the row from the bottom bar.
  • Key, on both voices' More tabs — pins that voice to one note.
  • Crush, on both voices' More tabs. Sample rate reduction.
  • A bypass box on the Pitch, Filter and Duck tabs.

Changed

  • The More tab's knobs are bracketed into named runs.
  • Reset window size is now an item in the right-click scale menu.

Fixed

  • Turning Rate to a faster division while a note is held speeds the pulse up immediately, rather than waiting up to four beats for the slower rate's already-scheduled pulse to arrive first.
  • Sweeping a free-running Rate in Hz now accelerates smoothly, rather than lurching the other way on some pulses.
  • Spread no longer eats headroom under FM.
  • Noise follows Spread, widening with the voice instead of staying centred.

v0.5.0 · 2026-08-22

Added

  • Grid divisions on the shape editor's strip, X and Y, between the zoom bar and Length. They rule the gridlines and set what Shift+drag snaps to.
  • Random Steps and Random Curves, at the foot of the shape editor's right-click menu. Both draw one step per X division with every level on a Y gridline.

Changed

  • Letting go of the tempo wheel puts the pattern back on the grid at once, rather than walking it there over the following bar. Walk back, at the bottom of the wheel's right-click menu, ramps it there over the next bar instead, with Quantize on.
  • Bending a curve in the shape editor is Option+drag (Alt on Windows), not Cmd.

v0.4.0 · 2026-08-20

Added

  • Duck Rate, in Length's slot on the Duck tab. The duck window is no longer fixed at a quarter note.
  • A lock at the right-hand end of the envelope strip fits the plot to the part of the gate that plays, and refits it as you drag Length.

Changed

  • The Rate list now runs 2/1 to 1/128. Presets and sessions come back on the division they were saved with; Rate automation drawn in an earlier build now points at a different one.

Fixed

  • With Quantize on, letting go of the tempo wheel puts the pattern back on the DAW's grid over the next bar.
  • The scope's gridlines line up with the trace while the transport is stopped.
  • The keyboard toggle dims evenly when hovered.

v0.3.0 · 2026-08-18

Added

  • A tempo wheel, at the right-hand end of the keyboard. Right-click for range.
  • Reverb on the Kick — Amt, LP and Size on its More tab, ducked by the Bass's Sidechain curve.
  • Reverse, the new R in the header. It records one Kick rate period and plays it back reversed.
  • MIDI output — one short note per Kick pulse, for sidechaining anything to the Kick. In Live this is VST3 only; other DAWs untested.
  • A combined scope. The + between the two scopes merges them into one panel showing the summed output.
  • The pitch and tempo wheels can be automated or mapped to hardware. Map both to one macro for a tape stop.

Changed

  • The scope gridlines sit on real pulses, which fixes dotted rates and unquantized notes. With Restart on Kick on, the Bass's lines start again on each Kick pulse, so you can see what it's doing.
  • The Wave tab dims the part of the cycle a pulse never reaches — a low note under a fast Rate doesn't get all the way round the drawn waveform.
  • Muting the Bass no longer greys out its Duck tab while that curve is still shaping the Kick's reverb.
  • A level meter's clipping cap grows with the overshoot.
  • The logo button lists every shortcut and mouse gesture in the plugin.
  • The scope grows with the window.

Fixed

  • Hovering an envelope point takes the same colour as hovering a line.

v0.2.1 · 2026-08-13

Added

  • Free-running rate. Each voice's Rate can run in Hz instead of a note division — right-click the Rate knob to switch.

v0.2.0 · 2026-08-13

Added

  • An on-screen keyboard, with a pitch wheel. Pitch bend from the host or a hardware wheel works now too.
  • A bottom bar, and a tooltip on every control.
  • Bend, on the Bass — a continuous pitch offset, on its More tab.
  • Interface scale, 75–200%. Right-click the background or the resize corner.

Changed

  • Dragging the window bigger grows the shape editor, rather than magnifying everything. Double-click the resize corner to reset it.
  • Value boxes are typeable and draggable. Hold Cmd to fine-adjust, double-click to reset.
  • Length moved out of the knob column onto the shape editor's control strip.
  • Dragging a knob hides and pins the cursor.

Fixed

  • Resonance self-oscillated slightly off-pitch.
