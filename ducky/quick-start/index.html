Quick Start

Get DucKy running.

What you need

macOS
macOS 11 Big Sur or later, on Apple Silicon or Intel. VST3 and AU.
Windows
64-bit Windows 10 or later. VST3.
DAW
Any DAW that loads VST3 or AU plug-ins. There’s no AAX version, so it won’t run in Pro Tools.

macOS

Open DucKy-1.0.0-macOS.pkg and follow the installer.

Windows

Unzip DucKy-1.0.0-Windows.zip and copy the whole DucKy.vst3 folder, not the files inside it, into your VST3 folder, usually:

C:\Program Files\Common Files\VST3

Windows will ask for administrator permission.

If DucKy doesn’t show up in your DAW, install Microsoft’s Visual C++ Redistributable (x64) and rescan.

Find it in your DAW

Rescan your plug-ins and search for DucKy. In Live, hold Option (Mac) or Alt (Windows) while you click Rescan.

In Logic Pro and GarageBand, use the AU. In any other DAW, use the VST3.

Duck your mix

  1. Put DucKy on what you want to duck.

    Load it on a track or group.

  2. In Live, set Slypork as a Control Surface.

    The first time you load DucKy, it installs a Live script called Slypork. Restart Live, then in Settings > Tempo & MIDI, set a Control Surface to Slypork. Without it, DucKy can duck early if plug-ins before it add latency. You only do this once.

  3. Pick what it ducks on.

    Tempo
    Ducks on the beat.
    MIDI
    Ducks on every note you route to it.
    KerBy
    Ducks on every kick from KerBy (kerby.slypork.net), with no routing. With more than one KerBy, right-click KerBy in DucKy and pick one. They’re listed by track name.

  4. Shape the duck.

    Depth sets how far it ducks, the rate how long, and Smooth rounds off the corners. Right-click the plot to pick a shape, or draw your own.

  5. Split it into bands if you want.

    Pick 2 or 3 under Bands, in the bottom bar. Each band has its own depth, rate and shape.

Updates and help

Your download link always has the latest version. Install it over the old one.

Something not working? Email dev [at] slypork [dot] net with your OS, DAW and what happened.
