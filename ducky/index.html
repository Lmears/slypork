DucKy

KerBy’s duck,
on anything.

Put it on any track or group and it dips the volume on the beat, on every MIDI note you send it, or on KerBy’s kick. Draw the shape of the dip and set how deep it goes.

Free · out 10 November, alongside KerBy macOS 11+ · AU / VST3 Windows 10+ · VST3

Trigger it

On the beat, on every note, or on the kick.

Follow the tempo

Ducks at the Duck Rate, from 1/1 to 1/16, in time with your DAW.

Follow the notes

Ducks on every note you send it, on any channel or pitch, even with the transport stopped.

Follow KerBy

Ducks on every kick from KerBy, with no MIDI routing. Shown where KerBy is installed.

Shape it

Draw the duck.

Start from Pump, Hold then rise or Soft recovery, or draw your own in the same editor as KerBy’s Duck tab. Depth sets how far it dips, and Smooth rounds off the curve.

Save your shapes as presets, as in KerBy.

Pair it with KerBy

Duck your mix to the kick.

Put DucKy on a group and choose KerBy, and the group pumps with the kick, even as you change its Rate or turn up Glitch.

Set it up in Ableton Live
