DucKy

KerBy’s duck,
on anything.

A simple volume shaper. Use KerBy’s kick to duck any track or group, with no MIDI routing.

Get notified at launch

Free · out 10 November, alongside KerBy macOS 11+ · AU / VST3 Windows 10+ · VST3

Trigger it

On the beat, the notes or the kick.

Follow the tempo

Ducks at the Duck Rate, from 1/1 to 1/16.

Follow the notes

Ducks on every MIDI note you send it.

Follow KerBy

Ducks on every kick from KerBy, with no MIDI routing.

Shape it

Draw the duck.

Pick a preset or draw your own. Depth sets how far it dips, Smooth rounds it off.

Pair it with KerBy

Duck your mix to the kick.

Put DucKy on a group and it pumps with KerBy’s kick, even as you change Rate or turn up Glitch.

Set it up in Ableton Live
